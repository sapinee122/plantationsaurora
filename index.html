About

A working farm, not a middleman.

Every bale we sell comes off our own fields in Quebec, Canada, cut, raked, and baled by us. We can tell you when a load was cut, how it was cured, and how it was stored, because we did the work.

We built Plantations Aurora around supplying that kind of straight answer to buyers who order hay by the load rather than the bag.

Quality

Dust-free hay you can build an order around.

Cutting and baling on our own schedule means we take hay off the field at the right moisture and get it under cover before quality slips. Every bale is checked before it leaves the farm, so what arrives is clean, green, and dust-free.

We grow every type your animals need, from timothy and alfalfa to clover and mixed grass, in small square, large square, or round bales. One farm can fill your whole order, at the same standard load after load.

Wholesale

Delivered to your door in the US and Europe.

We sell wholesale: full loads for feed operations, equestrian facilities, retailers, and resellers across the United States and Europe. We ship direct from our farm to your door.

Get in touch with your volume and timeline, and we'll work out a supply arrangement that fits your operation.
