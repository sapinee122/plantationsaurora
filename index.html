About

A working farm, not a middleman.

Every bale we sell comes off fields we cut, rake, and bale ourselves. That means we can speak to how a load was made — when it was cut, how it was cured, how it was stored — because we did the work.

We built Plantations Aurora around supplying that kind of straight answer to buyers who order hay by the load rather than the bag.

Quality

Hay you can build an order around.

Cutting and baling on our own schedule means we take hay off the field at the right moisture and get it under cover before quality slips. Bales are checked before they ever leave the farm.

If consistency matters for your operation, that's what we're set up to deliver — the same standard, load after load.
